# OKR: JellyBox Deformation Refinement

## Objective
Establish the JellyBox as a benchmark for high-fidelity, shader-physics integration in the workspace.

## Key Results
1.  **KR1**: 100% elimination of unrequested "hover color" changes.
2.  **KR2**: Implement directional "leaning" deformation that perfectly aligns with Rapier velocity vectors.
3.  **KR3**: Ensure zero floor clipping even under maximum (1.5x) impact intensity.
4.  **KR4**: Achieve sub-millisecond shader update overhead (measured via FPS stability).
