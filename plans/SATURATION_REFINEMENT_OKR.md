# OKR - Color Picker Saturation Refinement

## Objective
Harmonize the aesthetic of the spatial color picker rings through saturation standardization.

## Key Results
- **KR1**: Reduce saturation of `ringOuter` (large ring) from 75 to 60.
- **KR2**: Confirm and maintain saturation of `ringInner` (mid ring) at 60.
- **KR3**: Ensure no regressions in performance or accessibility.
