# Objectives & Key Results (OKR) - Jelly Cube Simulation

## Objective: Deliver an Industry-Leading Interactive Jelly Simulation on the Web
Achieve a tactile, responsive, and visually perfect soft-body simulation that is stable across all platforms and performs flawlessly.

### Key Results:
1. **Performance**: Maintain a stable 60 FPS on both desktop and high-end mobile devices in the viewport.
2. **Robustness**: 100% rendering success rate (no black squares or crashes due to mobile floating-point FBO driver limitations).
3. **Fidelity**: Achieve realistic stretching from the exact mouse grab point, combined with post-release wobbling and momentum-based squishing.
4. **Lighting**: Ensure specular reflections and environmental refractions warp realistically as the cube's geometry deforms.
